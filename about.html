About

Connecting Assyrian physicians, nurses, pharmacists, dentists, and health professionals around the world.

Our mission

The Assyrian Medical Professional Society (AMPS) is a professional network for Assyrian health-care professionals and students. We foster collaboration, mentorship, and service.

What we do

  • Maintain a directory of members across specialties and countries
  • Host educational events and networking gatherings
  • Support students pursuing careers in medicine and health sciences
  • Partner on community health initiatives

Get involved
